# Clinical question corrections: fourth batch

Fourteen IDs were revised and released from the hold after checking their specific flagged defects. Question IDs and historical records remain unchanged; previous scores reflect earlier wording and are not recalculated. These changes are educational and do not certify other items.

- **m2-mnt-107, m2-mnt-109:** distinguish the minimum fasting threshold for the named one-step OGTT approach and individualize gestational-diabetes carbohydrate planning. [ADA 2026 pregnancy standards](https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/49/Supplement_1/S321/163918/15-Management-of-Diabetes-in-Pregnancy-Standards).
- **m2-mnt-135:** identify absorbed peritoneal-dialysate glucose as an energy source without imposing a universal protein target. [National Kidney Foundation](https://www.kidney.org/sites/default/files/nutrition_and_peritoneal_dialysis.pdf).
- **m2-mnt-126:** use early oral feeding as tolerated in mild acute pancreatitis. [ACG 2024](https://webfiles.gi.org/links/journals/AJG-Clinical-Guidelines-Highlights-Acute-Pancreatitis-2024-FINAL.pdf).
- **m2-renal-021, m2-renal-023:** distinguish total fluid from effective arterial volume in cirrhosis and avoid requiring normal BUN in isonatremic dehydration. [AASLD](https://www.aasld.org/liver-fellow-network/core-series/back-basics/back-basics-low-sodium-high-stakes-back-basics), [UW Medicine sodium reference](https://dlmp.uw.edu/test-guide/view/NA).
- **m2-mnt-028:** withhold enteral feeding during uncontrolled hemodynamic instability pending resuscitation. [ASPEN/SCCM](https://doi.org/10.1097/CCM.0000000000001525).
- **m2-gf-pnc-02, m2-gf-pnc-05:** correct glucose infusion rate interpretation and distinguish cycling from increasing total dextrose. [ASPEN PN resources](https://nutritioncare.org/news/parenteral-nutrition-therapy-assessment-tools-and-guidelines/), [cyclic PN study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11100349/).
- **m2-gf-pcos-08:** qualify acanthosis as an association, not a diagnosis, and remove routine insulin-assay instruction. [International PCOS guideline 2023](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/pdf_file/0003/3379521/Evidence-Based-Guidelines-2023.pdf).
- **m2-gf-wic-06:** qualify authorized food forms by state program. [USDA](https://www.fna.usda.gov/wic/food-packages/regulatory-requirements).
- **m2-gf-mh-06, m2-gf-bar-04:** treat suspected Wernicke encephalopathy urgently and do not delay emergency glucose for hypoglycemia. [ASAM](https://downloads.asam.org/sitefinity-production-blobs/docs/default-source/quality-science/the_asam_clinical_practice_guideline_on_alcohol-1.pdf), [ASMBS](https://asmbs.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/ASMBS-Nutritional-Guidelines-2016-Update.pdf).
- **m2-mnt-172:** assess coexisting causes and treat the inflammatory condition rather than recommending erythropoietin universally. [NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/blood-diseases/anemia-inflammation-chronic-disease).
